Name the move

Is the move in front of you a partnership, or an acquisition? They are evaluated differently.
A partnership multiplies what you already have — reach, capability, credibility — without ownership changing hands. An acquisition absorbs what someone else built, and with it their people and their culture. Pick the one that describes the move you're actually weighing.
Your choice sets which evaluation runs in Section 3.

3

Evaluate the move

A partnership has two failure modes to check. An acquisition has two preconditions to clear.
Choose partnership or acquisition in Section 2 above to run the right evaluation.
Most partnerships fail in one of two ways. Work through both honestly before you commit — a partnership that fails these isn't a smaller opportunity, it's a distraction.
Failure mode 1 · The infrastructure lesson
Can you actually carry the partnership's volume?
Channel and referral partnerships fail when the partner sends business you can't deliver on. The partnership exposes every weakness in your delivery infrastructure — at scale, and in public.
Failure mode 2 · The differentiation lesson
Does the partnership keep you distinct — or blur you?
Co-delivery partnerships fail when the partner's brand and yours merge into something generic. The client can no longer tell what they're buying, or why it's you.
An acquisition is only worth pursuing once both preconditions are met. Be honest — treat "Almost" as a no. This gate exists to stop an expensive mistake before it's signed.
